Conference Topics

  1. Home > Conference Topics

Welcome to the 13th FBL International Conference titled: “Sustainable Growth, Digital Innovation and Market Dynamics: Bridging Business and Law”

This academic event offers researchers and practitioners the opportunity to explore a wide range of topics at the intersection of economic policy, business innovation, and sustainability. We invite you to explore our comprehensive list of conference topics outlined below, highlighting the diverse and relevant areas that will drive research, dialogue, and collaboration.

Join us in shaping the future of economic and sustainable development through insightful contributions and collective expertise!

Sustainable Growth and Economic Transformation

  • Sustainable development models in emerging European economies
  • Green growth and climate-resilient economic policies
  • Productivity, competitiveness, and long-term growth
  • Labor markets, human capital, and skills development
  • Regional development and economic convergence
  • The role of public policy in sustainable economic transformation

Digital Innovation and the Digital Economy

  • Digital transformation of businesses and markets
  • Innovation ecosystems and entrepreneurship
  • E-commerce, digital platforms, and consumer behaviour
  • Artificial intelligence, big data, and business analytics
  • Digital skills, education, and workforce adaptation
  • Legal challenges of digitalization and new technologies

Finance, Accounting, and Sustainable Investment

  • Sustainable finance and green investment
  • ESG reporting and non-financial disclosure
  • Financial markets development and stability
  • Public finance, fiscal policy, and debt sustainability
  • Accounting standards and financial transparency
  • Investment climate and access to finance for SMEs
  • Green accounting and digital banking

Market Dynamics, Competition, and Regulation

  • Market structures and competition in emerging economies
  • Regulatory frameworks for fair and efficient markets
  • State aid, competition policy, and market supervision
  • Consumer protection and market transparency
  • Informal economy, market distortions, and compliance
  • Market responses to global and regional shocks

Law, Governance, and the Rule of Law

  • Legal certainty and business environment
  • Commercial law and investment protection
  • Public administration reform and governance quality
  • Anti-corruption policies and regulatory enforcement
  • Regulatory capacity, supervision, and enforcement challenges
  • Criminal Justice Reform
  • Transport law, contracts, liability and insurance
  • Reform of Intellectual Property Law
  • Social Security law and alignment with EU practices

European Policies, National Reforms, and Priorities

  • Alignment with EU Green and Digital priorities
  • Economic and legal reforms in enlargement countries
  • Institutional capacity building and policy implementation
  • EU programs and funding instruments
  • Comparative perspectives on European policy reforms
  • National strategies and sectoral reforms

Marketing, Innovation, and Consumer Markets

  • Conscious consumer behaviour and sustainability
  • Innovation and green technologies in markets
  • Legal frameworks and consumer protection
  • Corporate ethics, ESG, and responsible business
  • Circular economy and sustainable production
  • Digitalization, transparency, and informed consumers

Artificial Intelligence, Markets, and Regulation

  • AI adoption in businesses and SMEs in emerging European economies
  • Market dynamics and competition in AI-driven sectors
  • AI applications in finance, accounting, marketing, and management
  • Data-driven business models and algorithmic markets
  • EU Legal frameworks governing artificial intelligence
  • Data protection, privacy, and intellectual property in AI contexts
  • Labor market transformation and skills requirements.
  • Ethical challenges and social implications of AI adoption

Interdisciplinary and Emerging Issues

  • Green and digital transitions: economic and legal implications
  • Crisis management and economic resilience
  • Demographic change, migration, return migration and labour markets
  • Gender equality and inclusive economic growth
  • Education, research, and innovation policies
  • Future challenges for emerging European economies
  • AI and the future of research
